# Commercial Validation: Port Harcourt Solar Installers

**Created:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** hypothesis selected, first five prospects audited, permission-based outreach not started
**Owner:** Jephtah

## Hypothesis

Port Harcourt solar and inverter companies have valuable enquiries coming through websites, Google, phone, and WhatsApp. A small improvement to the enquiry path, mobile experience, quote request flow, or follow-up process is commercially more valuable to them than a generic website redesign.

The first offer to test is:

> I will identify and repair the biggest online enquiry leak for your solar business within 48 hours, then show you how to measure the enquiries it generates.

This is a validation hypothesis, not a promise of rankings, leads, or revenue.

## Why this market is worth testing

- The Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission describes businesses, hospitals, universities, and factories as active buyers of commercial and industrial rooftop solar, driven partly by unreliable grid power and diesel costs. [NIPC energy sector overview](https://nipc.gov.ng/sectors/energy)
- The IEA's 2026 electricity outlook describes Nigeria's solar PV growth as being on a sharp upward trajectory and forecasts continued electricity-demand growth. [IEA Electricity 2026](https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/88bb4abe-0308-42f9-a46f-c776a9528522/Electricity_2026.pdf)
- Public search results show a sufficiently deep local market with companies serving Port Harcourt across residential, commercial, industrial, and battery-storage segments.

## Research checklist for every prospect

Record the following before contacting anyone:

- Business name and exact website
- City/service area
- Decision-maker or role, if publicly available
- Primary enquiry channel: phone, WhatsApp, form, or email
- One checkable observation with URL and timestamp
- Whether the site works on a phone
- Whether the main CTA is visible without hunting
- Whether a quote/load-assessment request can be completed quickly
- Whether trust proof is close to the CTA
- Whether the site routes enquiries to a human or a dead end
- Contact confidence and source
- Preferred first channel
- Outcome and objection after contact

Do not copy a contact address into a campaign until it has been manually checked against the company's own current website or verified business profile.

## Validation script

The detailed channel scripts and outcome definitions are in [outreach-playbook.md](outreach-playbook.md).
The first three copy-ready permission requests are in [first-contact-queue.md](first-contact-queue.md); they are prepared but not sent.

First contact should request permission to send the observation instead of attaching a long audit immediately:

> Hi, I was checking how solar companies in Port Harcourt handle quote enquiries and noticed one small friction point on [company]'s website. I made a short note showing what a potential customer experiences on mobile. Should I send it here?

If permission is granted, send a five-sentence audit:

1. Say what the business does well.
2. State the exact observation and source.
3. Explain the likely enquiry friction without inventing lost-lead numbers.
4. Suggest the smallest practical fix.
5. Offer a 10-minute walkthrough or a paid 48-hour repair.
