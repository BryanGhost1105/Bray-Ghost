# 48-Hour Enquiry-Path Repair Pilot

**Audience:** a Port Harcourt solar installer after a short audit walkthrough  
**Status:** experimental offer for the first paid pilot  
**Price test:** **₦75,000 fixed** — ₦37,500 to start and ₦37,500 after handover

This is a narrow implementation pilot, not a promise of rankings, leads, or revenue. The price is a starting test for validating willingness to pay, not a claim about the market rate.

## What the client gets

Within 48 hours of receiving access and the first payment:

1. A 20-minute walkthrough of one observed enquiry-path issue.
2. A mobile check of the relevant page, CTA, form, phone link, WhatsApp handoff, or routing step.
3. One small repair, chosen from the observed issue:
   - clearer call-to-action;
   - tap-to-call or WhatsApp handoff;
   - focused enquiry form or routing adjustment; or
   - one landing-page handoff that makes the next step clearer.
4. Before-and-after screenshots and a short handover note.
5. A simple check that the repaired path reaches the agreed destination.

## Boundaries

Included is one website or enquiry-path repair. Hosting, paid software, ad spend, copywriting for a full site, full redesigns, ongoing SEO, CRM replacement, and additional pages are excluded and can be quoted separately.

There is no guarantee of search ranking, enquiry volume, sales, revenue, or a particular conversion rate. The measurable promise is limited to completing the agreed repair and demonstrating the path works.

## Acceptance and payment

- The client accepts the written scope and supplies the required access or approval.
- The first 50% is paid before implementation begins.
- The remaining 50% is due after the repair is demonstrated and the handover note is delivered.
- If the observed issue cannot be safely changed with the available access, the scope is paused and revised in writing before more work is done.
